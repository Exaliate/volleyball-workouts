    How the pieces fit: four training blocks

    Translated and condensed from the caption of the @trainingfor_better_ reel, which lays out a full volleyball S&C framework.

    Core

    • Anti-extension, anti-rotation, anti-side-bend
    • Low-back stability
    • Rotational strength and power
    • Flexion power

    Stability

    • Braking: vertical, horizontal, lateral
    • Shoulder: cuff and fast eccentric control
    • Hip: hamstring eccentrics, single-leg
    • Ankle: static and dynamic

    Strength

    • Order: eccentric → isometric → concentric → transfer
    • Max strength feeds power (force × velocity)
    • Sport-angle strength last

    Power

    • Olympic lift derivatives
    • Depth jumps: high box for force, low for speed
    • Med ball: rotation and flexion
    • Band-resisted crossover and shuffle starts

    Sources

      3D demos are simplified mannequin animations to show the shape of each movement, not exact technique.
